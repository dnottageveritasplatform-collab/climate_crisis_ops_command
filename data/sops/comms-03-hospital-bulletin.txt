COMMS-03-DEMO - Hospital Partner Transport Bulletin Template

COMMS-03 Overview:
- Standard bulletin for hospital transport liaisons during storm escalation.
- Dual HITL required: NEMT supervisor AND hospital liaison before send.
- With multiple hospital partners (e.g. Princess Margaret + Doctor's Hospital): triple HITL — NEMT supervisor + PMH liaison + Doctor's liaison.
- Never auto-send; draft only until both approvers sign off.

COMMS-03 Level 2 Prepare:
- Subject: Transport Advisory - Level 2 Prepare - New Providence
- Include: affected corridors, P1 trip count, estimated delay window.
- Attach 24h manifest excerpt tagged PRIORITY.
- Flag dialysis and oncology patients explicitly.

COMMS-03 Level 3 Restrict:
- Subject: URGENT Transport Restriction Notice - Level 3
- State CORR-01 and CORR-02 closure/restriction status.
- List held trips by priority; require liaison callback for P1 exceptions.
- Reference NEMT-CRISIS-001 dual approval workflow.

COMMS-03 Distribution:
- Primary: hospital transport liaison (Princess Margaret Hospital — public partner)
- Primary: hospital transport liaison (Doctor's Hospital — private partner)
- CC: NEMT dispatch supervisor, on-call clinical coordinator
- Channel: secure email or operator portal; no public SMS at Level 3
