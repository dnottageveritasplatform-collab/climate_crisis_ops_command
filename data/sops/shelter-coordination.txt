SHELTER-01-DEMO - Shelter Coordinator Coordination Protocol

Shelter Coordinator Role:
- Extended HITL persona at Level 2+ — approves shelter routing drafts, not shelter operations authority.
- Coordinates with NEMT dispatch and hospital liaisons on evacuee transport to accepting shelters.
- Read-only capacity feed from operator JSON adapter; no direct bed assignment in CCOC.

Level 2 Prepare:
- Review shelter capacity signals before releasing P1 trips through restricted corridors.
- Flag near-capacity shelters in action pack shelterRoutingBrief for liaison review.
- Cross-reference CORR-01/CORR-02 status with National Gymnasium and parish hall staging zones.

Level 3 Restrict:
- Hold elective shelter transfers until fleet logistics confirms alternate routing.
- Require shelter coordinator HITL approval alongside NEMT supervisor and hospital liaisons.
- Log shelter routing decisions in audit trail with SHELTER-01 citation.

Verification:
- Shelter coordinator approves draft only — COMMS-03 hospital bulletins remain triple/extended HITL gated.
- No auto-routing to shelters without human approval on action pack drafts.
